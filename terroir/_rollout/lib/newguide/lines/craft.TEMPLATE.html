Almost everything sold to visitors as a craft experience is a demonstration. The question here is narrower: is the craft actually alive here, is there a real teacher, and can you enrol?

The four bars

Every entry below is scored against the same test, and where a place fails a bar the failure is printed rather than glossed.

How these are judgedthe testA named master · a teaching lab · open enrolment · at the source.

1 · A named, still-practising master. A real person you can name, still doing the work — not “local artisans”.

2 · Dedicated to the craft. A place whose purpose is the making and the teaching, not a side demonstration.

3 · Open enrolment. You can book or enrol as an outsider. This is the bar almost everything fails, and the one that matters most.

4 · At the source. The craft is alive in that place, in its own working scene.

The rarest thing is not talent. It is a master who runs an open door instead of a closed workshop.

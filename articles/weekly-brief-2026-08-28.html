Weekly Brief, 28 August 2026: The Cold, Not the Bag

First brief of the thread: the two calls still open on the board, the llampuga season that opened on Tuesday, and three cold-chain numbers that pass the brigade test.

The week the cold chain got its numbers. Four pieces crossed the desk between the llampuga boats going out on Tuesday and the last supplier page being checked on Friday, and every one of them came back to the same three-word rule for the galley: the cold, not the bag. This is the first brief of the thread, so the scoreboard below starts empty and honest.

No call has closed yet — this is the first brief, and the two calls on the board are still inside their windows. The butter call (Butter at Four Euros a Kilo): fill the freezer while the EU quotation sits at €398 per 100 kg, 45% under last year — its window closes in the week of 5 October 2026, and that brief prints the quotation, held or missed. The fish call (The Cheap Fish Go First): sardine and anchovy up at least a quarter on 2025 by 2028, bluefin flat on 48,403 t — first reading next June, when the GFCM advice lands. Nothing to settle; two things to keep score on.

With the board set, here is the single change that earns the rest of the read.

Put the probe thermometer in your pocket on provisioning day and read every chilled crate at the van's tailgate, not in the galley: 5 °C or below comes aboard, anything warmer goes back on the note.

This week

Llampuga: the season opened on Tuesday, with a cap on every boat

The one fish the island cooks from now to November went legal on 25 August, and the rules that shape its price are published.

The Balearic government opens the llampuguera season on 25 August and closes it on 31 December, with a daily limit of 150 kg of llampuga per boat and a fleet-wide ceiling of 127 tonnes; the 2023 season landed 99,486 kg for €592,376, about €6 a kilo at first sale, and the press reproduction of this year's release puts the 2025 season at 114,459 kg for €646,273 across 35 Mallorcan boats. The galley consequence: a spoken special from the first week of September, never a printed line, and a price that the daily cap keeps from collapsing.

The fish are caught around capcers — rafts of palm and mastic branches anchored to make shade — because dolphinfish gather under floating cover; the encircling net closes on what the shade has collected.

Source: Govern de les Illes Balears, season release (season dates, 150 kg/boat, 127 t TAC, 2023 landings), 2024 — official; 2025 figures via Última Hora, 23 August 2026, reproducing the Govern's text — press, one step from the source.

3.3 °C: the FDA's line for vacuum-packed raw fish

The number that makes a chamber sealer a food-safety decision, not a gadget one.

Clostridium botulinum type E — the marine one — grows and makes toxin from 3.3 °C, and in a vacuum bag it has the oxygen-free package it wants. For vacuum-packed refrigerated raw fish where refrigeration is the only barrier, the FDA's guide says the temperature should be kept below 38 °F (3.3 °C) from packing to consumption. A galley fridge at 5 °C is inside the growth range.

Bagged raw fish lives on ice in the fish drawer or in the freezer, never on a 5 °C shelf; open the bag before it goes back to ordinary refrigeration.

Source: FDA, Fish and Fishery Products Hazards and Controls Guidance, June 2022, Chapter 13 and Table A-1 — regulatory guidance, primary.

The bag is not the preservative; the cold is.

One day: what a fridge at 6–10 °C does to a fish's clock

The same guide carries the table that turns a warm fridge into a deadline.

The FDA's cumulative-exposure table gives a fishery product seven days at −0.4 to 5 °C before Listeria monocytogenes is the concern, and one day at 6–10 °C; Vibrio parahaemolyticus gets two hours at 22–27 °C and one hour above 27 °C. On an August quay, a crate that leaves the Llotja at five in the morning is spending the table's second row before it reaches the boat.

A fridge reading 7 °C on the morning log is not “a bit warm”: on this table it has cut a week of fish life to a day.

Source: FDA, Fish and Fishery Products Hazards and Controls Guidance, June 2022, Table A-2 — regulatory guidance, primary.

Twelve days against nine: what a vacuum actually buys at 4 °C

A counted comparison, so the sealer's promise has a number on it.

A Polish team packed carp fish balls under vacuum with a tabletop chamber machine and, for comparison, in air, and held both at +4 °C for fourteen days — 28 sets, two methods by seven sampling days by two replicates, sixteen samples a set. Sensory life ran to 12 days under vacuum against 9 in air, and fat-oxidation markers were lower in the vacuum-packed samples; both stayed inside the microbiological limits the authors used for the whole fortnight. Three days of quality, bought by taking the air out, on a product held cold.

A vacuum buys days of quality on food held at 4 °C; it buys nothing, and risks something, on raw fish held above 3.3 °C — the two items above are one rule.

Source: Kaliniak-Dziura et al., Effect of Packaging Method (Under Vacuum vs. Air) on Quality and Shelf Life of Carp Fish Balls Stored at Fridge for 14 Days, Molecules, 2026 — peer-reviewed, n = 28 sets × 16 samples, processed product not raw fillet.

Checking Palma's suppliers this week meant reading a bakery's own history page: Fornet de la Soca was started by Tomeu and Maria José in the 2008 crisis, moved into the old Forn des Teatre in 2018, and describes itself as a project to recover the island's memory of taste. The one thing

The week folds down to a single line.

Read the crate at the tailgate, keep bagged fish under 3.3 °C, and treat a 7 °C shelf as a one-day shelf — the cold, not the bag.

That is the keep. Three threads stay open on the board for next week.

Butter: the EU weighted-average quotation, week by week to the week of 5 October — a quotation still at or under €398 then says the window was early; a run of higher weeks says it held. Sardine and anchovy: the Spanish wholesale sardine price (Observatorio de Precios, Mercasa average) — above €4.50/kg in July–August 2027 firms the call, back under €3.00 falsifies it; and the Gulf of Lions mean sardine length in Ifremer's summer survey — above 135 mm two summers running kills it. Palma: the count line on the provisioning-day card — services run, units found above 5 °C — if any come back, they print here, boat unnamed.

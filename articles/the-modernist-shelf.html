The Modernist Shelf: Four Books Worth Their Weight, and the Six That Cost Nothing

Modernist Cuisine ships at 23.6 kilos and the hydrocolloid collection every chef quotes weighs nothing. Which books actually print the grams per litre, the time at temperature and the oxygen line for sous vide, spheres, foams, texturizers and nitrogen, which editions exist in English, where each one is sold, and why the free PDF of a Disfrutar book does not exist.

Read Baldwin and the Khymos collection tonight for nothing, buy Modernist Cuisine the day the boat has a shelf that takes twenty-four kilos, and close any page offering a Disfrutar book as a free PDF: there is no such file, only a trap.

What you need, and why:

  • Modernist Cuisine: The Art and Science of Cooking — The Cooking Lab, 2011, £395 from Phaidon for all five techniques under one index: 2,438 pages where sous vide, gels, foams, spheres and nitrogen share one set of units.
  • Disfrutar, Volumes 1 to 3 — Abalon Books, 2021 to 2025, $350 a volume in English for what spherification became after elBulli: volume 2 alone carries 96 recipes and 50 QR codes that open the videos.
  • Sous-Vide Cuisine — Joan Roca and Salvador Brugués, Montagud, 2003 in Spanish, 2005 in English, €110 from the Celler shop for the pouch: the sous-vide book a restaurant kitchen wrote a decade before the circulator reached the galley.
  • Handbook of Molecular Gastronomy — CRC Press, 2021, library price for the mechanism: Baldwin wrote its sous-vide chapter, Barham its cryogenics chapter, and spherification gets three.
Buy if

Buy the four if you cook from books and the boat has the shelf; read the six free texts first whatever you decide, because every dose, temperature and oxygen line on the paid shelf is already printed in one of them.

Why It Works

A technique is a set of numbers. The books that print them are the ones you can cook from at sea.

Every one of the five techniques comes down to a dose, a temperature and a time, and the test of a book is whether it prints them. The free Cooking Issues primer gives gelatine at 1.0 to 1.5 per cent, alginate at 0.5 to 1 per cent and a calcium bath at 0.8 to 1.5 per cent; a restaurant monograph that gives you the plate and not the percentage has given you a photograph.

The reason the dose matters more than the recipe is that the gels are not alike. Agar sets between 32 and 45 °C and does not melt until 85 to 95 °C, so an agar gel survives a hot pass; gelatine melts at body temperature, so it does not. A book that prints set and melt points with the percentage beside them scales from six covers to sixty; a book that only plates does not.

The trade is weight against depth. Khymos gives the doses in 138 pages and nothing of the hand on the siphon; Modernist Cuisine gives the hand, the history and the physics for 23.6 kg of locker; the Barcelona monographs give complete recipes and thin science. The numbers themselves are in the sister piece on what each preparation holds at sea; this one is about where they were first printed.

What the Shelf Actually Holds

No charter trial here. This is a reading audit: every record checked against the publisher, every free text downloaded and its licence read.

What worked

  • The free texts print the doses. Khymos is 138 pages of hydrocolloid percentages, recipe by recipe; Baldwin's guide carries the time-at-temperature tables the paid books cite.
  • The Girona book still stands. Roca and Brugués printed the method in 2003; Montagud keeps a bilingual edition in print.
  • Disfrutar shows the hands. Volume 2's QR codes open videos of the techniques, the one thing a book could never do before.

Where it struggled

  • Language. Candy, Compartir and Caldos are Spanish-first; Compartir has no English edition; Candy sells in New York, in Spanish, at $190.
  • Print runs. The 2003 La cocina al vacío is out of print; Caldos has sold out in New York; Natura (2008) is out of stock at Albert Adrià's own shop.
  • Weight and price. Modernist Cuisine is £395 and 23.6 kg; three Disfrutar volumes in English pass $1,000; the handbooks are priced for libraries.

Watch for: a search for any Disfrutar volume returns a dozen pages offering it as a free PDF or EPUB. None is the book: they are pirated scans or bait, the royalties are the three chefs', and a yacht's network is the last place to open an unknown file. Every link in this piece goes to the publisher, the author's shop or a named bookseller.

The Rest of the Shelf

The Barcelona books, the free library, and what to do about the two under everything.

FreeTexture: A hydrocolloid recipe collectionKhymos · 138 pages · PDF

What you give up: every photograph of a finished plate; this is a table of doses, contributed recipe by recipe.

Best for: dosing agar, gellan, alginate and xanthan in grams per litre before you own a single Barcelona book.

Martin Lersch, a chemist in Norway, began the collection in 2007 and released version 3.0 in February 2014 under a Creative Commons licence. Download it from khymos.org.

FreeA Practical Guide to Sous Vide Cooking, and the Cooking Issues primersBaldwin · Arnold · online

What you give up: the restaurant plate, and any binding.

Best for: the time-at-temperature tables, and the three primers on low-temperature cooking, hydrocolloids and liquid nitrogen with their doses and safety rules.

Baldwin is a mathematician whose guide at douglasbaldwin.com grew into a book, a peer-reviewed review and a Handbook chapter; Arnold wrote the primers at cookingissues.com while teaching sous vide at the International Culinary Center.

FreeThe open textbooksLibreTexts · BCcampus · CC licences

What you give up: they are written for apprentice cooks and bakers, so the modernist chapters are short.

Best for: the vocabulary under the techniques: viscosity, gelatinisation, coagulation, with a chapter each on non-traditional thickeners and on low-temperature cooking.

British Columbia's cook-training committee published Modern Pastry and Plated Dessert Techniques in 2015 under a licence any school may reprint; it is at opentextbc.ca, the chemistry at LibreTexts.

FreeThe safety textsFDA · FSA · EIGA · PDF

What you give up: nothing to cook.

Best for: the lines no monograph prints: fish in a pouch below 3.3 °C from packing to plate, ten days for a vacuum pack unless another barrier is proven, 19.5 per cent oxygen in the room.

The FDA guide is at fda.gov, the 2016 FSA guidance at foodstandards.gov.scot, and the gas-industry document at eiga.eu.

FreeThe maker's sheets and the Harvard lecturesSosa · Harvard SEAS · online

What you give up: the sheets sell a brand's powders; the lectures are ninety minutes each.

Best for: Sosa's one-page Texture Wheel and its foam and gel booklets for the dose by product; Harvard's Science and Cooking lectures for the physics spoken by the chefs themselves.

Sosa's downloads are at sosa.cat; the lectures at sciencecooking.seas.harvard.edu, whose textbook is the one paid book on this card.

Buy laterTapas, Tickets Evolution, CandyAlbert Adrià · RBA · €35, €35, €85

What you give up: the science; these are the plates of one Barcelona group, 2011 to 2021, with the recipes.

Best for: the siphon and the sweet side of texturizers, where Candy's 512 pages go further than any English pastry book in print.

Tapas and Tickets Evolution exist in English and ship signed from albertadria.com at €35 each; Candy is Spanish only, €85, by David Gil, a pastry assistant at Tickets in 2012 and head of the group's desserts three years later.

Spanish onlyCompartir, and CaldosPlaneta Gastro 2017 · Montagud 2015

What you give up: an English edition of Compartir, which does not exist; Caldos is bilingual but sold out at its New York stockist.

Best for: Compartir for the fifty dishes the Disfrutar trio cooked in Cadaqués before Barcelona; Caldos for the stock pot, the one technique no modernist book treats as a technique.

Camarena's juices were already the spine of his Valencia menu in 2014, when the local press called them complex seasonings; the book followed in November 2015 and weighs two kilos. Compartir is at planetadelibros.com; Caldos at Casa del Libro.

DeeperHandbook of Hydrocolloids, and the two under everythingElsevier 2020 · McGee 2004 · This 2006

What you give up: recipes. The handbook is a chapter per hydrocolloid, agar to gellan to gelatine, at a library price.

Best for: the chef who wants to know why the gel failed. Under all of it sit McGee's On Food and Cooking and Hervé This's Molecular Gastronomy, the two books every author above cites.

The elBullifoundation is still writing the encyclopaedia this shelf grew from: the Bullipedia runs to more than thirty 500-page volumes since 2013, and the seven-volume elBulli 2005–2011 catalogue is sold from the foundation's own store.

Skip itThe free PDF of a book that is not freeAny search result that says descargar gratis

What you give up: nothing, because the file is not the book.

Best for: no galley. Abalon publishes Disfrutar with the restaurant itself; Montagud has kept the Roca book in print since 2003; the price of the paper pays the next volume.

In the search run for this piece, the Disfrutar volume 2 query returned more pirate and bait pages than bookshops. The bookshops are named above; the bait is not.

Tested at sea is owed: the shelf has been read, not yet cooked from on a passage, and the first galley that does will say so in the Weekly Brief.

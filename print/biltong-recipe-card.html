Biltong — Salt, Vinegar, Six Days of Moving Air

Never cooked: the overnight vinegar bath and a controlled hang are the kill step, the salt only seasons. Vinegar well past 3% of the batch, at least six days on the hook, done by weight.

Yield: 12 × 125g (from 3 kg raw) Active: 40 min Total: 7 days Box: 20–26°C (68–79°F) · 45–60% RH Done: ≥6 days + 50% loss
Where it was bornSouthern Africa. Dutch bil (rump) + tong (strip). Said to begin with salt-and-sun drying older than the settlers, who allegedly added vinegar and Cape spices; the Voortrekkers dried it up to two weeks for the wagons.
Eaten, authenticallyIn the hand: a whole stick, cut across the grain with a pocket knife as it is eaten. Beef or game; salt, vinegar and coriander the constants.
How it’s used nowA boxed, fan-dried snack sold wet, medium or dry by weight lost. Lab-validated in 2020: 5-log Salmonella kill with no heat, in 6–8 days.

The ratio — trimmed raw beef = 100% Salt 2.2% · Vinegar 15% · Coriander 1.5% · Sugar 1% · Pepper 0.5% · Bicarb 0.1% · Cure #1 0.19%. Salt 1.7–2.7% made no difference to the kill; vinegar at 2% failed, 3–4% passed (Karolenko 2020). 0.19% cure = 79 mg/kg nitrite ion, under the EU 80.

Kit: 0.01 g scale for the cure · non-reactive trays · stainless S-hooks · biltong box or cabin with a small fan · hygrometer · kitchen scale for the test strip · marker

Method

  1. Cut with the grain. Firm 30–45 min in the freezer, silverskin off. Strips 2–2.5 cm thick, 4–5 cm wide, 20–30 cm long. Same thickness throughout: the thickest sets the date.
  2. Toast, crack, mix. Dry-toast the coriander until fragrant, crack coarsely. Mix with salt, sugar, pepper, bicarb and the weighed cure. EU since 9 Oct 2025: 80 mg/kg nitrite ion ingoing in non-heat-treated meat. The packet’s 2.5 g/kg = 105 — over. 1.9 g/kg = 79. — Reg. (EU) 2023/2108
  3. Season, rest. Press the cure into every face. Cover, 1 h at ≤5°C.
  4. Vinegar bath, overnight. Vinegar + Worcestershire over, turn to coat, cover. 12–18 h at ≤5°C, turned once. Validated: 16–20 h at 5°C in 10% vinegar → 5-log kill after 6 days’ drying. 2% vinegar failed. — Karolenko et al., Microorganisms 2020
  5. Drain, weigh, hang. Pat, never rinse; re-dust coriander. Mark a test strip, write its day-0 weight. S-hooks, 3–4 cm apart, touching nothing.
  6. Dry ≥6 days. 20–26°C, 45–60% RH, fan always on. Never down before day 6, whatever the scale says. Too hot, too fast or too dry seals a crust over a wet centre (case-hardening). At the end, surface aw 0.80, inside 0.89–0.90. — Jones 2017; Karolenko 2020
  7. Read it by weight. Loss = (day 0 − today) ÷ day 0. ~35% wet (fridge, eat in a week) · ~50% medium · 60%+ dry.
Done: test strip half its day-0 weight, at least six days hung; outside dark and dry, cut face deep red and firm right through, no wet sheen; medium bends before it snaps.
Serve: keep sticks whole; slice to order, thin, across the grain.
One cure, three batches Wagyu 500 g, BMS 4–6: coriander light, cider vinegar, cure recommended (antioxidant for the fat); full bath, full 6 days, stop at 40–45% loss; fridge, eat within 2 weeks. Dock sale 10 kg: bath fixed at 16 h, cure every batch, release at ≥6 days and ≥50%, logged; register first (Reg. 852/2004), ask the local authority before the first bag.

Elevation

Tier 1 — No Extra Time
  • Malt vs cider — malt for the classic sour, cider for a cleaner one
  • Chilli — +3 g flakes per kg in the cure
Tier 2 — Worth It
  • Smoke — +5 g smoked paprika per kg
  • Game — kudu, springbok or venison loin for the beef
Tier 3 — Restaurant
  • Wagyu — sliced paper-thin, laid out as charcuterie
  • Two tiers — guests the wagyu, crew the silverside, one cure

Troubleshoot

Hard out, wet inCase-hardening → cooler, slower fan, more humidity
Stalls at 40%Room too humid → dehumidifier or A/C
White bloomDry, wipes off with vinegar: salt. Fuzzy or coloured: mould, discard
Too saltySalt concentrates as water leaves → take down at 50%

Charter Prep & Storage

At seaDry in A/C or by a dehumidifier: air wetter than the stick puts water back
KeepWhole sticks, paper bags, cool and dry; vacuum-pack and freeze for longer
FridgeWet biltong and wagyu, always
NeverLess vinegar, a hot oven or the engine room, down before day 6
